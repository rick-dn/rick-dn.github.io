Overview

Robofruit is an autonomous strawberry-harvesting robot developed at the University of Lincoln. I designed its vision pipeline: 3D instance segmentation and localisation of strawberries, feeding a patented picking head that removes occlusions and cuts the stem without touching the fruit. The system was selected for demonstration at COP26.

Modular Autonomous Strawberry Picking Robot

Journal of Field Robotics, 2023

A modular, reconfigurable harvesting system for dense strawberry clusters, combining a patented picking head (2.5 degrees of freedom) with a perception system that localises strawberries, detects their key points and picking points, and determines ripeness.

  • Ripeness detection with 95% accuracy
  • Harvested 87% of detected strawberries, with an 83% success rate on pluckable fruit
  • Tested on three strawberry varieties in a commercial farm and a research farm

Picking Point Localisation, Ripeness and Weight Estimation

ICRA 2022

The perception work behind Robofruit: two new strawberry datasets annotated with instance masks, picking and grasping key-points, pluckability, size and weight, and a Detectron2 (Mask R-CNN) key-point model for picking-point and orientation detection.

  • Key-point detection localises picking and grasping points directly from the image
  • A novel baseline for weight estimation that outperforms several state-of-the-art deep networks
