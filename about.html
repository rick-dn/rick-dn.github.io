Dr Bappaditya (Rick) Debnath

Robotics & Perception Engineer

Contact Me ↗

I build autonomous systems that see, from research to flight. I'm a robotics engineer with a PhD in computer vision, and I currently work on safety-critical autonomy for drones flying beyond visual line of sight. My work centres on perception and navigation: visual-inertial odometry, SLAM, GNSS-denied flight, state estimation and GNC. Along the way I've worked on 3D computer vision, medical imaging AI and agricultural robotics, across industry and academia (Oxford, King's College London, Lincoln). Before research, I spent my early career building enterprise software and infrastructure.

What I Work On

  • Perception & Navigation

    Visual-inertial odometry (VIO), visual SLAM and GNSS-denied navigation for UAVs, with optical flow and camera–IMU fusion.

  • GNC & State Estimation

    Guidance, navigation and control: EKF/ESKF sensor fusion, GPS-loss fallback and fault injection on ArduPilot.

  • UAV Autonomy & Safety

    Layered flight stacks on ROS 2, onboard failure modes for BVLOS, and SITL/HITL simulation.

  • 3D Computer Vision

    3D reconstruction, camera pose estimation, structure from motion, ICP and point tracking.

  • Edge AI

    Real-time detection and tracking on NVIDIA Jetson and Raspberry Pi, with TensorRT and INT8 models.

  • Medical & Agri AI

    Medical image segmentation, diffusion-based synthetic data and in-silico trials; vision for robotic fruit picking.
